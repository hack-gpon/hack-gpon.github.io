The firmware of the [8311 community](https://pon.wiki/) for the [MaxLinear PRX126](/chipset-maxlinear-prx) XGS-PON sticks: the [firmware builder](https://github.com/djGrrr/8311-was-110-firmware-builder) applies the community changes to the original firmware image.

- [Install the 8311 community firmware on the WAS-110](https://pon.wiki/guides/install-the-8311-community-firmware-on-the-was-110/) ([source](https://github.com/up-n-atom/PONwiki/tree/main/docs/posts/install-8311-community-firmware-on-the-bfw-solutions-was-110))

::: warning
Vendor forks of the 8311 firmware are not supported by the 8311 community.
:::
