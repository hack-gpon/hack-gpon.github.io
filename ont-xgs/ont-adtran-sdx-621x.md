The SDX 621X is a 10G XGS-PON single family unit (SFU) ONT of the Adtran SDX 600 series. Unlike the [SDX 621](/xgs/ont-adtran-sdx-621), which has the optics on board, the 621X has a pluggable SFP+ WAN port for the XGS-PON optics, two Ethernet ports (10GE and 1GE) and two POTS ports.
