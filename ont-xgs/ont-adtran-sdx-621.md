The SDX 621 is the 10GbE XGS-PON single family unit (SFU) ONT of the Adtran SDX 620 series. The 1GbE variant is the [SDX 621i](/xgs/ont-adtran-sdx-621i), the variant with one POTS port is the 621-V (1287837Fx). It is succeeded by the [SDX 631](/xgs/ont-adtran-sdx-631) of the SDX 630 series.

## Specifications

From the ADTRAN 621/621-V datasheet (2019):

|                       |                                                                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PON                   | XGS-PON (ITU-T G.9807), BOSA on board, Class B+ and Class G optics, DBA |
| Ethernet              | 1x RJ-45 100M/1/2.5/5/10GBase-T, 10 Gbps symmetric throughput, auto negotiation, MDI/MDIX |
| Ethernet services     | 802.1D bridging, 802.1x, 8 queues (SP/WFQ), VLAN 0-4095, Q-in-Q, VLAN translation, 9k jumbo frames |
| Video                 | Mediaroom and Minerva bridged video models, IGMP v2/v3 snooping       |
| Management            | OMCI (ITU-T G.988 and Open OMCI), Mosaic Activate, 15 min rolling counters, dying gasp |
| LEDs                  | Power, LAN, Optical, Voice                                            |
| Operating temperature | 0 °C ~ +40 °C                                                         |
| Relative humidity     | 5% ~ 95%, non-condensing                                              |
