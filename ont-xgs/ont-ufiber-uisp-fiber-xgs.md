The UISP Fiber XGS is the XGS-PON ONU of the Ubiquiti UISP Fiber line, with one XGS-PON port and one 10GbE RJ45 LAN port, supporting bridge and router mode. It is managed with the UISP application.

::: warning
According to Ubiquiti, the ONU works only with the UISP Fiber XGS OLT and cannot be used with third-party OLTs.
:::
