The FiberTwist XGS2110 is a Layer-2 XGS-PON ONT with one 10GE LAN port. Like the GPON [FiberTwist G2110C](/ont-genexis-ft-g2110c), it is installed by twisting it on the Genexis fiber termination unit (FTU) or bracket. The 2.5GE variant is the [FiberTwist XGS2110-2.5G](/xgs/ont-genexis-ft-xgs2110-2.5g).
