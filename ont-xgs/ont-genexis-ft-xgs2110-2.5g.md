The FiberTwist XGS2110-2.5G is a Layer-2 XGS-PON ONT with one 2.5GE LAN port. Like the GPON [FiberTwist G2110C-2.5G](/ont-genexis-ft-g2110c-2.5g), it is installed by twisting it on the Genexis fiber termination unit (FTU) or bracket. The 10GE variant is the [FiberTwist XGS2110](/xgs/ont-genexis-ft-xgs2110).
