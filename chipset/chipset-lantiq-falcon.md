The Lantiq Falcon GPON SoCs (PEB98035, PEB98036). The firmwares are built either on the Lantiq SDK with OpenWrt, see [Lantiq SDK (OpenWrt)](/firmware-lantiq-openwrt), or on other systems like eCos.

Some sticks have a physical EEPROM, on the others the SoC emulates it on the I2C interface: check the device page.
