The chipset pages group the devices by SoC family. The device pages link their family from the `Chipset` row of the "Hardware Specifications" table, and the family pages list their devices automatically:

```md
| Chipset | [Realtek RTL9601D](/chipset-realtek-rtl960x) |
| System  | Linux 3.18 (Luna SDK 3.3)                    |
```

The `Chipset` row holds only the chip; the operating system and the SDK (e.g. Luna SDK, Saturn SDK, OpenWrt) go in the `System` row.
