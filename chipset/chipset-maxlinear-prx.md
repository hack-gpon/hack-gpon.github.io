The MaxLinear PRX SoCs (PRX126, PRX321). MaxLinear took over the home gateway SoCs of Intel, formerly Lantiq: the PRX126 is the XGS-PON successor of the [Lantiq Falcon](/chipset-lantiq-falcon). The XGS-PON sticks run an OpenWrt based MaxLinear SDK.

For the block diagram of the PRX126 see [BFW Solutions WAS-110](/xgs/ont-bfw-solutions-was-110#architecture).
